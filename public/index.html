How we find your product details

Everything the converter reads from your store before it builds your static site.

For every product we read the same fields your storefront already shows shoppers, plus the metadata search engines need:
  • Name, short & full description
  • Regular price, sale price and currency
  • SKU, stock status and quantity
  • All gallery images, in full resolution
  • Variants and options (size, color, material, etc.)
  • Categories, tags and collections
  • Weight/dimensions and related or cross-sell products
  • SEO slug, meta title and meta description, when available
Every image attached to a product — including gallery and variant-specific images — is downloaded, compressed, and converted to modern formats like WebP automatically, so your static site loads fast without any manual image work.
All variants are captured with their own price, stock level, SKU and image, and rendered as selectable options on the generated product page — so a shopper can still pick "Red / Medium" the same way they could on your original store.
Yes. Stock status and quantity are read directly from your store, so sold-out items are automatically marked unavailable instead of showing an "Add to cart" button.
We connect with your Consumer Key and Consumer Secret over the official WooCommerce REST API, using read-only permissions to fetch products, variations, categories and media. Nothing is ever written back to your store.
Each platform has its own official store API — Shopify's Admin API, Wix's Stores Catalog API, and Squarespace's Commerce API. We use your access token to read the same catalog data (products, variants, collections and image URLs) in a read-only way, so the process feels identical no matter which platform you're on.
Your credentials are only used for the duration of the conversion request and are never stored. Read-only access is all the converter ever asks for — it can't edit products, change settings, or place orders on your store.
Live behavior like checkout, payment processing and account logins can't be "static" — those stay with your original store or a checkout provider you link to. The cart itself still works using your browser's local storage, so shoppers can add items and check out through your existing payment flow.
It depends on your catalog size and image count, but most stores with a few hundred products finish in well under a minute — you can watch each stage (connect, export, generate, package) live in the progress panel above.
